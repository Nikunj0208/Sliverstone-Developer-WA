# Silverstone Developers — WhatsApp Message Style Guide

## 1. Overview & Principles
The Silverstone Developers WhatsApp automation uses the exact wording and emojis provided in the client marketing document (`raw/✨ SILVERSTONE DEVELOPERS ✨.docx` and assets). The content is optimized strictly for mobile WhatsApp UI/UX:
- **Same-to-same wording and emojis** from the source
- **Scannable mobile blocks** (clean line breaks instead of run-on text)
- **WhatsApp formatting** (`*bold*` headings and labels)
- **No loss of factual information or original tone**

---

## 2. Heading & Formatting Rules
- **WhatsApp Bold Formatting**: Use `*TEXT*` for project titles, section headings, and field names (e.g. `*⚜️ RAJMAHEL ⚜️*`, `*Starting Price*`).
- **No Markdown `#` Headings**: WhatsApp does not render `#` tags as headings. Use WhatsApp-native `*bold*` formatting.
- **Section Headers**: Use the client's original styled headers (e.g. `*⚜️ SPRINGHILL HOMES ⚜️*`, `🏡 *PREMIUM ROW HOUSE & PLOTS* 🏡`).

---

## 3. Mobile Spacing Rules
- **Separated Lines for Key Bullets**: Grouped information like road dimensions or connectivity points are placed on separate lines for effortless mobile reading.
- **Intentional Blank Lines**: Use a blank line between introductory headings and bullet lists.
- **Avoid Cramped Text**: Multi-point lines are broken down into clean, individual visual items.

---

## 4. Emoji Usage Rules
- **Strictly Preserve Source Emojis**: All emojis from the client's marketing material (`✨`, `⚜️`, `👑`, `🏰`, `🏡`, `📍`, `✅`, `📐`, `💰`, `🔑`, `🏛️`, `🌴`, `🚪`, `🌅`, `🏪`, `👨‍👩‍👧‍👦`, `🛣️`, `🌐`, `⚓`, `☀️`, `📏`) are preserved exactly as provided.
- **Visual Anchors**: Emojis precede key features to help users quickly scan project benefits on smartphone screens.

---

## 5. Price & Size Display Rules
- Prices and plot sizes are displayed with their original emojis and wording:
  ```text
  ✅ 💰 Starting Price: પ્લોટ માત્ર ₹47 લાખથી શરૂ.
  ```
  or
  ```text
  ✅ 💰 Starting Price: પ્લોટની કિંમત ₹72.80 લાખથી શરૂ.
  ```
- No added promotional jargon or unverified urgency claims.

---

## 6. Bilingual Gujarati & English Content
- Maintain the original natural Gujarati phrasing and English terminology.
- Preserve proper spacing between Gujarati and English terms.
- No joined words or corrupted OCR characters.

---

## 7. Interactive Buttons & CTA Rules
- Every completed step offers clear interactive options:
  - Main Welcome: `View Projects`, `Chat`, `Call`
  - Project Details: `Download Brochure`, `View Plans`
  - Confirmation screens return to the canonical menu with zero dead ends.

---

## 8. Link & Media Delivery Rules
- Google Maps and Walkthrough Video links are displayed with clear visual headings (`📍 *PROJECT LOCATION*`, `🎥 *PROJECT WALKTHROUGH*`).
- Missing videos or floor plans are handled with professional notices directing the user to the sales concierge.
